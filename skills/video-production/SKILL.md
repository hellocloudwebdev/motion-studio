# End-to-end video production

1. Parse the brief, audience, message, duration and deliverable formats.
2. Identify missing inputs and separate assumptions from facts.
3. Write a production brief and narrative beats.
4. Build a storyboard with scene intent, frame durations and asset needs.
5. Encode the plan in Canonical Video IR.
6. Validate and route before any renderer is invoked.
7. Run QA, repair and final delivery checks.
