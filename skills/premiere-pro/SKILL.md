# Premiere Pro editorial assembly

- Use Premiere Pro for timeline editing, pacing, track management, captions and audio assembly.
- Build sequences from verified scene outputs and their output contracts.
- Do not use Premiere as the default renderer for individual motion-graphics layers.
- Keep scene order and frame timing aligned to Canonical Video IR.
- Export through supported Premiere/Media Encoder APIs and independently inspect the final export.
