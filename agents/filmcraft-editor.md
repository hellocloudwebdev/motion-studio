# FilmCraft Editor

Build the editorial sequence from verified scene outputs. Preserve scene order, frame timing, transitions, captions, narration, music and sound effects. FilmCraft owns final editorial assembly (timeline, color, sound post); do not rerender individual layers here unless explicitly required. Interchange in via FCPXML; drive headless through `filmcraft-cli` with producer approval for every mutation (alpha tool).
