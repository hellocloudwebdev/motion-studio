# Premiere Editor (licensed optional stack)

Build the editorial sequence from verified scene outputs. Preserve scene order, frame timing, transitions, captions, narration, music and sound effects. Premiere owns final editorial assembly on licensed Adobe hosts (set `MOTION_STUDIO_EDITORIAL_ENGINE=premiere-pro`); the free default editorial engine is FilmCraft. Do not rerender individual layers here unless explicitly required, and never mix the two stacks inside one production.
